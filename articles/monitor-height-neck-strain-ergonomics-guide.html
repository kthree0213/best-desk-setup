Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my monitor arm keep tilting down?
This is caused by "Torque Overload." Even if your monitor is under the weight limit, the depth of curved screens pulls the center of gravity forward. You need a High-Torque Heavy Duty Pivot specifically designed to handle the leverage of ultra-wide curves.
What is the correct height for a 32-inch monitor?
For 32-inch screens and larger, ignore the "top at eye level" rule. Instead, align your eyes with the top third or center of the screen. This prevents you from slouching to see the taskbar and reduces neck strain during long working sessions.
