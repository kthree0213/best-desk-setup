Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do M-series MacBooks support dual external monitors natively?
M1/M2/M3 Pro and Max chips support multiple monitors natively. Base M1/M2/M3 chips only support one natively. The M4 base chip supports dual displays with the lid closed.
Q: Is there a difference between USB4 and Thunderbolt 4?
Practically, they provide similar 40Gbps speeds, but Thunderbolt 4 has stricter certification requirements for Mac. Always opt for Thunderbolt 4 to guarantee dual display and power delivery compatibility.
