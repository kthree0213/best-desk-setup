Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BenQ ScreenBar Halo worth the extra cost over Quntis?
Yes, if you use a curved monitor or value a wireless desk aesthetic. The BenQ Halo includes a patented mounting clip for curved screens and an ambient backlight that reduces eye strain in dark rooms. For standard flat monitors with basic utility needs, a Quntis alternative provides 80% of the benefit at a fraction of the cost.
Does the BenQ wireless controller have lag?
According to user testing and video reviews, the BenQ Halo wireless dial has a 1–2 second wake-up delay to conserve battery life. Users who prefer instant responsiveness may find physical touch controls on the bar more satisfying for rapid adjustments.
Can I use these light bars on an ultra-wide curved monitor?
The BenQ ScreenBar Halo is specifically designed with a patented accessory to fit curvatures from 1000R to 1800R. Budget bars can struggle with stability or light distribution on high-curvature panels. The Halo is the safest bet for high-end ultrawides.
What should I avoid when buying a monitor light bar?
Avoid light bars that lack an asymmetrical optical design. These cheaper models often shine light directly onto the screen, causing glare that worsens eye strain. Always check for a CRI of 90 or higher for professional color work.
