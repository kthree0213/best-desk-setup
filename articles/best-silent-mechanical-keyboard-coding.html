Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are silent mechanical keyboards worse for typing speed?
No. Modern silent tactile switches provide the same actuation force and tactile bump as normal switches, simply absorbing the acoustic impact. Most developers maintain or improve their WPM (Words Per Minute).
Q: Can I manually make my loud coding keyboard silent?
You can reduce the noise by adding O-rings to the keycaps or filling the empty case space with neoprene foam. However, if your keyboard is hot-swappable, the most effective method is simply replacing the switches with \"Silent Peach\" or \"Silent Alpaca\" variants.
